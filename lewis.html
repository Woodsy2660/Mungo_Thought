Where he's from · Yenda, NSW

Raised where the food grows.

Yenda sits just outside Griffith, in the Riverina: irrigation country, with vineyards and orchards on every road out of town.

Growing up there, you knew the time of year by what was on the table. Stone fruit meant summer. Citrus meant winter. Nobody needed telling what was in season, because it was growing down the road. [Add a memory from growing up in Yenda: a family table, a garden, a first job.]

Why Mungo Thought

Cooking seasonal food again.

Eating the way we're meant to: what's ripe, what's close, and nothing flown in from the other side of the world to fill a gap in the menu.

Mungo Thought is how Lewis brings that back to the table. There's no fixed kitchen and no set menu: just a night in someone else's room, cooked from whatever is best that week, in Sydney and back home around Griffith.
