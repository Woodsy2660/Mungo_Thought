Where he's from · Yenda, NSW

Raised where the food grows.

Yenda sits just outside Griffith, in the Riverina: irrigation country, with vineyards and orchards on every road out of town.

Growing up there, you knew the time of year by what was on the table. Stone fruit meant summer. Citrus meant winter. Nobody needed to be told what was in season, because it was growing down the road. [Add a memory from growing up in Yenda: a family table, a garden, a first job.]

That's what Lewis is cooking his way back to: seasonal food again, and eating the way we're meant to. What's ripe, what's close, and nothing flown in from the other side of the world to fill a gap in the menu.

What's ready

The menu is written around what's at its best that week, not the other way round. When something's over, it comes off.

02

From close by

Fish from the boat in Sydney, fruit and vegetables from growers around Griffith. The shorter the trip, the better it tastes.

03

The whole thing

Whole fish, whole birds, the tops and the peel. Cooking all of it is how it used to be done, and it's how we eat at the pass.
