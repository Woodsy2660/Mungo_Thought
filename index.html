What is Mungo Thought

Pop-up dinners and kitchen collaborations across Sydney and Griffith, cooked by the season.

Lewis takes over a kitchen for a night, whether it's a restaurant on its night off, a wine bar or a courtyard, and cooks a short menu from whatever is best that week.

There's no fixed address and no set menu. The produce decides: what came off the boat in Sydney, what the growers are picking around Griffith, what's at its peak right now. Every dinner is a one-off, and when it's gone, it's gone.
