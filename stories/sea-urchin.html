Sea urchin, split by hand

Uni comes in for a short window. When it does, we build the menu around it.

The ingredient · No. 014

What
Sea urchin roe
From
[Diver], [coast]
Season
Dec – Feb
On at
Long Lunch, 17 Jan

[Sample copy.] The urchins come up in a crate with the seawater still on them. You crack them over the sink with the back of a spoon, tip out the dark insides, and what's left is five orange tongues sitting in the shell like a little hand.

We don't do much to it. A slice of toast from the wood oven, cultured butter, the uni laid on cold, a few drops of lemon and that's the plate. Any cleverer and you lose what you paid for.

The season is short, so when it's here we cook around it, not with it.

At the Long Lunch it goes on the first plate, before anyone's had a drink, so it's the thing you remember. The rest of the menu follows it: something cured, something from the fire, something sweet and cold.
